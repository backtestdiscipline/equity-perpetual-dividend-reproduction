The bank documents are preliminary notices about underlying securities for Canadian depositary receipts, not the issuers' own ex-date notices. Their stated ex-dates corroborate the event dates, subject to their own possible errors. A record date is not treated as proof of an ex-date. AAPL and META issuer pages and Microsoft issuer page were inspected; only Apple was captured locally. The Apple exchange-label discrepancy and GOOGL Class A ex-date source's weaker provenance remain explicit caveats.

[Binance's methodology page](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/7ced719b5e9a4859a1864c2fe657309f), viewed 2026-11-11, says it was published 2026-04-14 and updated 2026-10-01, after all five events. It currently specifies -D/M with M the execution-time mark. The direct retrieval returned an empty response, so no local original bytes of this page are claimed. The paper's June 29 reference to that page is not a preserved version of its contents. We found no dated pre-event copy establishing the then-current formula or showing whether the historical API mark is the same quantity as M. The numerical fit to -D/(API mark-D) is therefore evidence about the returned records, not proof of the actual historical calculation or a rule change.
