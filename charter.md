# Study charter

- Question: Do the five Binance special funding entries in Seo et al. match the paper's formula?
- Kind: retrospective reproduction; same-data exploration was conducted before freeze.
- Target: reproduce the five rate comparisons, not the proof or economic trading claim.
- Non-goals: independent dividend validation, account-level cash flows, historical rule reconstruction, cross-venue comparison, and profitability.
- Sources: public Binance API responses and arXiv v1 HTML, retained locally in ignored data. Public accessibility is not a grant of unrestricted redistribution; no source bytes are committed. No confidential inputs or external AI calls are required by the study.
- The paper's dividend amounts are transcribed inputs, not independent issuer evidence. Current Binance methodology states a different formula; timing and historical interpretation remain open.
